# Reusable knowledge

Discovery, research, evaluation and generic repository workers are read-only.
Implementation workers may modify explicitly authorized paths in isolated worktrees;
independent executable verification and a current revision audit remain required.
Codex sessions are stored by the SDK/CLI, while SQLite retains identifiers, handoffs,
quality reports and evidence. Interrupted work has an uncertain outcome; inspect or
resume it before making a duplicate assignment. Never assume a running process
survived a restart. Accepted research is not proof of a deployed improvement.

Reuse knowledge only with its provenance, scope, freshness and classification.
Facts, assumptions, estimates, rejected hypotheses and invalidated findings remain
distinct. A rendered chart or a confident summary is not a substitute for source
data. Preserve uncertainty and the next validation trigger in durable handoffs.
